Rakeeza — Construction Management ERP

A field-operations mobile app with an Arabic right-to-left interface to help organize construction projects, workers, equipment, attendance, and expenses.

FlutterDartGoogle Sheets APIPDF ReportingOffline-Friendly Workflows

Project Overview

Rakeeza is a construction operations application designed to bring everyday project records into a single mobile workflow. The application includes interfaces for project operations and equipment management, helping organize field activities and related records.

Problem Statement

Construction teams need to keep track of workforce attendance, equipment usage, maintenance activities, and project expenses. Managing these records across separate processes can make daily follow-up and reporting more difficult.

Proposed Solution

Rakeeza organizes construction-related workflows through a mobile application with an Arabic RTL interface. The app brings together workforce records, equipment tracking, and expense-related workflows, with connected data handling and reporting functionality.
